Trip Count by Hour — Perfectly normal pattern

Dead hours at 3–5am (expected)

Avg Fare by Day — Suspiciously flat
All days show ~$16–17 average with almost no variation. Saturday is very slightly higher which makes sense. But this flatness tells you the extreme outliers are inflating all days equally — once you clean the impossible trips, you'll likely see more natural variation.
